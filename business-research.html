Choosing the right source

Sort each research question into the type of source that could best answer it.

Company reportsProvide an overview and analysis of a company, including its organisational structure, industry position, financial performance and other key aspects.
Industry reportsExamine a specific industry, including its performance, market size and trends, key products or services, and major players.
Sustainability or impact reportsOutline a company’s stated ESG commitments and performance.
Scholarly journal articlesProvide academic analysis integrating theory and empirical evidence.
News or government sourcesReport current events, regulation and outside views.
i

Company and industry reports build context. Scholarly journal articles add theory.

Boolean or natural language search?

Business Source Premier offers two ways to search. Sort each situation, then try one.

Boolean search

You choose keywords and join them with AND and OR.

Natural language search

You type a question, and AI builds the search (EBSCO calls this AI-assisted search).

Searching for grey literature

Grey literature is published outside commercial publishing, often by governments, international organisations and NGOs. It is usually available online as a PDF and can help establish context through sources such as SDG reports, policy documents and official data.
