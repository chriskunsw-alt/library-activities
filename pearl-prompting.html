Tool tips

  • scite.ai answers questions using research articles and lists its references.
  • Its Smart Citations show whether later papers support, contrast or simply mention a finding. Use this to see if a claim is disputed.
  • Ask a follow-up for the "Look again" step, for example: which studies disagree with this?
  • Scopus AI, from Elsevier, summarises answers from research indexed in Scopus and lists the references it drew on.
  • Its answers are only as wide as Scopus coverage, so search other databases too.
  • Open each reference in Scopus and read the abstract or full text before you cite it.
