Read an industry report

Industry reports give you figures, competitors and trends. Practise finding what matters, using a soy milk industry report as the example.

Find industry reports in trusted databases such as IBISWorld and MarketLine through UNSW Library.

1

Know your sources

Both are trusted, dated sources you can cite. Each has its strengths.

IBISWorld

Industry reports with key figures, major players and operating conditions. Strong coverage of Australian industries.

MarketLine

Industry and company profiles across many countries. Company profiles often include a SWOT analysis.

Why not just ask AI?

AI tools can give outdated or invented figures with no source. Industry databases tell you where the numbers came from and when.

i

Can't find a report called “soy milk”? Reports often cover a wider group. Try broader or related names, such as plant-based milk or non-dairy beverages.
